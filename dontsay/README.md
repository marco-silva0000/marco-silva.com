# Don't Say the Same Thing as Me: Anti-Hivemind Party Game

"Don't Say the Same Thing as Me" is a fast-paced multiplayer party game that turns common trivia on its head: instead of trying to match answers, your goal is to think of valid answers that NO ONE else will say!

---

## 1. Reference

### Game Specifications
- **Player Count**: 3 to 16+ players.
- **Round Duration**: 30–60 seconds.
- **Game Modes**:
  - `classic`: Standard collision. Unique valid answers score +100. Collisions get 0.
  - `harsh`: High stakes. Collisions lose -50 points.
  - `the_me`: One player is "The ME" and secretly sets the trap answer. Everyone else must avoid colliding with "The ME" or be eliminated.
- **Pacing Modes**:
  - `funnel`: Wide category $\rightarrow$ Medium category $\rightarrow$ Narrow category (Sudden Death).
  - `narrow`: High-collision narrow categories only.
  - `random`: Unconstrained random category mix.

### Scoring Breakdown
- **Unique Valid Answer**: `+100 points`
- **Obscurity Bonus**: `+50 points` (awarded to answers not found in common top-10 lists)
- **Collision / Clash**: `0 points` (in Classic mode) or `-50 points` (in Harsh mode)
- **Invalid Answer**: `0 points`

---

## 2. Explanation

### The Anti-Hivemind Psychology
In most party games (like Family Feud or Codenames), players are rewarded for thinking like the crowd. "Don't Say" forces second-order psychology:
- If the category is "Name an Italian dish", you know everyone thinks of *Pizza* and *Pasta*.
- Do you go slightly offbeat with *Risotto*?
- But what if three other players also thought *Risotto* was the clever middle-ground choice?
- You end up with second-order collisions on the "clever" answers, while the obvious answers might go untouched!

---

## 3. How-To Guides

### How to Play "The ME" Elimination Mode
1. In Room Settings, choose Game Mode: **The ME Elimination**.
2. Round starts: "The ME" is secretly prompted to submit their answer first.
3. Once set, the rest of the players submit their answers.
4. During the reveal, any player who matched "The ME" is eliminated from the competition.
5. Rounds continue with new categories until only one player survives to take the crown!
