📖 {% trans "How to Play Rank It! (ito)" %}

1. Secret Numbers: Every player receives numbered cards from 1 to 100 without duplicates.

2. Subjective Spectrum: A random theme defines a scale from 1 (the lowest/weakest) to 100 (the highest/strongest).

3. NO Numbers Allowed: You cannot say your numbers or do math hints! You express your number through hints matching the theme (e.g. for dangerous animals: 12 is 'a grumpy badger', 95 is 'a Godzilla with armor').

4. Play in Order: Anyone can play their card onto the central timeline when they feel their card is the next lowest remaining.

5. Clashes: If a played card is smaller than a previously played card, the group loses 1 Life (💔)!

6. Victory: Play all cards without running out of lives to clear the levels!
