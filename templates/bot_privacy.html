Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

The Homelab Telegram Bot is a private personal productivity assistant developed by Marco Silva. It integrates with Google Calendar to help authorized users view their personal schedule and add calendar entries directly via Telegram.

2. Information We Access

When you connect your Google Account, the bot requests access to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope. This access is used strictly to:

  • Read upcoming calendar events to present daily briefings and schedule summaries.
  • Create or update calendar events upon your explicit command in Telegram.

3. Data Storage & Security

We do not store your calendar contents on external servers. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely and encrypted in a private, self-hosted homelab environment with strict firewall isolation and access controls. No calendar data is retained beyond immediate display.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Your calendar data is never sold, shared, monetized, or transferred to third parties, advertisers, or external data brokers. It is used solely to provide personal assistant functionality to you.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You may revoke the bot's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions. Upon revocation, the bot will immediately lose all ability to access your Google Calendar.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact Marco Silva at mail@marco-silva.com.
