# Tutorial: Playing SmartN in Couch Pass & Play Mode

Welcome! In this tutorial, we will set up and play a game of **SmartN** in **Couch Pass & Play Mode**.

Most digital party games require every single player to pull out a smartphone, connect to Wi-Fi, and scan a QR code. But sometimes you are gathered around a living room couch or dinner table and just want to play immediately. In Couch Mode, a single smartphone acts as the physical "Smartbox" toy passed from person to person.

By the end of this lesson, you will know how to configure a single-device match, add couch players, take turns passing the device, manage risk with the push-your-luck point basket, and arbitrate trivia debates with direct tap overrides.

---

## What We Will Achieve

We will:
1. Create a SmartN game room on our smartphone.
2. Enable **Couch Pass & Play (Single Device)** mode.
3. Add three offline players: **Alice**, **Bob**, and **Charlie**.
4. Start Card #1 and pass the phone around the circle to play turns.
5. Experience push-your-luck mechanics: opening capsules, banking points, and avoiding a bust.
6. Use the **Direct Tap Outcome Override** to arbitrate a disputed trivia answer.

---

## Step 1: Create the SmartN Room on Your Phone

Let's begin by picking up the smartphone that will serve as our handheld Smartbox.

1. In your mobile browser, navigate to:
   ```
   https://marco-silva.com/games/create/?game=smartn
   ```
2. Enter the room setup details:
   - **Room Title**: Type `Living Room Trivia`.
   - **Language**: Select **English (en)**.
   - **Anti-Bot Question**: Enter the sum of the two numbers shown.
3. Tap **Create Room**.

Notice that the browser opens the SmartN lobby with our room code (e.g. `8E41C902`).

---

## Step 2: Switch to Couch Pass & Play Mode

In the lobby, we see two game modes: **Multi-Device Mode** (default) and **Couch Pass & Play Mode**.

1. Tap the large mode card labeled:
   ```
   🛋️ Couch Pass & Play (Single Device)
   ```
2. Notice the instant visual change:
   - A purple information toast confirms: `Switched to Couch Pass & Play Mode`.
   - The multi-device QR code and phone-join instructions slide away.
   - A prominent couch setup box appears: **Add Couch Players (Single Device Mode)**.

---

## Step 3: Add Our Couch Players

Now let's register the friends sitting with us on the couch. Because we are in Couch Mode, they do not need their own phones.

1. Locate the **Enter player name** input box in the couch setup panel.
2. Type `Alice` and tap **➕ Add Player** (or press Enter).
   - Notice Alice appears in the player list with a vibrant colored avatar badge and the tag `(Couch)`.
3. Type `Bob` and tap **➕ Add Player**.
   - Notice Bob appears next in the roster with a distinct contrast color.
4. Type `Charlie` and tap **➕ Add Player**.
   - Notice Charlie joins the roster.

We now have 3 couch players listed and ready to compete!

---

## Step 4: Start the Match

With our couch roster set, let's start the game.

1. Tap the green primary button:
   ```
   🚀 Start SmartN Match
   ```
2. Look at the screen. The match begins immediately with **Card #1**:
   - At the top of the screen, a bold banner announces whose turn it is:
     `👉 Current Turn: Alice`
   - Below the banner, we see the Question Card prompt:
     `World Geography: Which of these 10 countries border the Mediterranean Sea?`
   - In the center of the board, we see **10 covered capsules** numbered #1 through #10.
   - At the bottom, the scoreboard displays Alice's round basket: `Basket: 0 pts`.

---

## Step 5: Take Alice's Turn (Open a Capsule and Bank)

Hand the smartphone to **Alice**.

1. Alice reads the question aloud to the room:
   > *"Which of these countries border the Mediterranean Sea?"*

2. Alice inspects the 10 covered capsules and taps **Capsule #3**.
   - The capsule flips open with a crisp pop sound, revealing:
     `Italy — ✅ Borders the Mediterranean (+1 pt)`
   - Alice's basket counter increments: `Basket: 1 pt`.

3. Now Alice faces the push-your-luck choice:
   - She can tap another capsule to try for 2 points, but if she picks a wrong answer, she will bust, lose her basket point, and be out for the rest of this card.
   - Or she can bank her point safely.

4. Alice decides to play conservatively. She taps the green button at the bottom:
   ```
   📥 Pass & Bank (1 pt)
   ```

Notice what happens:
- Alice's 1 point is transferred permanently to her score on the leaderboard (`Alice: 1 pt`).
- Alice safely bows out for the rest of Card #1.
- The turn banner instantly advances: `👉 Current Turn: Bob`.

---

## Step 6: Take Bob's Turn and Use the Outcome Override

Hand the phone across the couch to **Bob**.

1. Bob wants to beat Alice. He inspects the remaining 9 covered capsules and taps **Capsule #7**.
2. Capsule #7 flips open, revealing:
   `Jordan — ❌ Does not border the Mediterranean (Bust!)`
3. Bob's screen flashes red: `💥 BUST!`. Bob's basket drops to 0, and he is marked as eliminated for Card #1.

### Arbitrating a Table Dispute (Host Tap Override)
Suppose Bob immediately protests: *"Wait, I meant Egypt, I tapped the wrong box on the screen!"* or the table agrees that a historical or geographical technicality deserves a pass.

In SmartN, you never have to restart a game or argue with rigid computer software:

1. Tap directly on the revealed **Capsule #7** row.
2. An interactive popup opens: **Override Capsule Outcome**.
3. Tap the green button:
   ```
   ✅ Mark as CORRECT (+1 pt)
   ```

Notice the immediate correction:
- Capsule #7 updates to a green checkmark.
- Bob's status is restored from *Busted* to *Active*.
- Bob is awarded +1 pt into his basket!

Bob happily smiles, taps **Pass & Bank (1 pt)**, and passes the phone to Charlie.

---

## Step 7: Complete the Round and View the Leaderboard

Hand the phone to **Charlie**.

1. Charlie taps **Capsule #1**:
   `Spain — ✅ Correct (+1 pt)`
2. Feeling bold, Charlie pushes his luck and taps **Capsule #5**:
   `Greece — ✅ Correct (+1 pt)`
3. With 2 points in his basket, Charlie taps **Pass & Bank (2 pts)**.

With all three players having passed or completed their turns:
- The round ends, and the **Round Summary** card slides onto the screen.
- The full answers for all 10 capsules are revealed so everyone can see what they missed.
- The match leaderboard updates:
  - **Charlie**: 2 pts
  - **Alice**: 1 pt
  - **Bob**: 1 pt
- Tap **Next Card ➡️** to proceed to the next trivia round!
