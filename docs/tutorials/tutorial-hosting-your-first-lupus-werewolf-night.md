# Tutorial: Hosting Your First Lupus (Werewolf) Night as Game Master

Welcome! In this tutorial, we will take on the role of the **Game Master (GM)** in *Lupus in Tabula* (Werewolf). 

Running traditional Werewolf requires memorizing secret cards, remembering who pointed to whom in the dark, and calculating complex votes. With the digital **GM Cockpit** on `marco-silva.com`, all of that stress disappears. The system tracks secret actions, calculates dawn casualties, and tallies votes automatically—leaving us free to focus entirely on storytelling and entertainment.

By the end of this lesson, you will comfortably moderate a complete 7-player Werewolf round: dealing roles, directing Night 1, delivering the morning dawn report, and running the village trial.

---

## What We Will Achieve

We will:
1. Create a Lupus game room and open the **GM Cockpit**.
2. Connect 7 players using the room QR code.
3. Select the **Standard 7-Player** role preset and deal secret role cards.
4. Orchestrate **Night 1** as players silently enter their secret actions on their phones.
5. Review the automated **Dawn Report** and announce the morning casualties to the village.
6. Moderate the daytime discussion and execute the village trial vote.

---

## Step 1: Create the Lupus Room

Let's begin by launching our game room from our laptop or tablet.

1. In your browser, open the room creation page:
   ```
   https://marco-silva.com/games/create/?game=lupus
   ```
2. Enter the room configuration:
   - **Room Title**: Type `Village of Millers Hollow`.
   - **Language**: Select **English (en)**.
   - **Password**: Leave blank for easy joining.
   - **Anti-Bot Question**: Solve the math challenge displayed on the screen.
3. Click **Create Room**.

Notice that because we created the room, the system recognizes us as the host and automatically redirects us to our command center:
```
https://marco-silva.com/games/lupus/<CODE>/gm/
```
*(Where `<CODE>` is your unique 8-character room code, such as `4B91D220`)*.

---

## Step 2: Explore the GM Cockpit

Take a moment to look at the **GM Cockpit** dashboard in front of us:

1. **The Circular Seating Map**: Notice the large virtual round table in the center of the screen. As players join, their seats will appear in a circle around this table.
2. **The Phase Control Bar**: At the top, we see the current phase badge (`⏳ Lobby`) and our primary director buttons.
3. **The Night Stage Director & Dawn Reviewer**: On the right side, panels stand ready to display incoming night actions and casualty calculations.

---

## Step 3: Connect 7 Players to the Village

Now let's connect our 7 village players using their smartphones.

1. Click the **Show QR Code** button in the top bar to display the large room QR code on our screen (or project it onto a living room TV).
2. Have our 7 friends point their phone cameras at the QR code. Their phones navigate to:
   ```
   https://marco-silva.com/games/4B91D220/
   ```
3. Each player enters their name and taps **Join Room**:
   - `Alice`
   - `Bob`
   - `Charlie`
   - `Diana`
   - `Evan`
   - `Fiona`
   - `George`

Look at our GM Cockpit table:
Notice that 7 colored seats pop up in real time around the circular table. Each seat displays the player's name and distinct avatar color.

---

## Step 4: Choose the Role Deck and Deal Roles

Now let's set up a balanced deck for our 7 players.

1. In the GM Cockpit's deck configuration panel, select the **Standard 7-Player** preset:
   - **2 Werewolves** (The evil pack hunting at night)
   - **1 Detective** (The Seer who inspects a player's aura each night)
   - **1 Doctor** (The Bodyguard who protects one player from attack each night)
   - **3 Villagers** (Innocent townsfolk who deduce and vote by day)
2. Verify that the total card count equals our player count (7 cards for 7 players).
3. Click the primary green action button:
   ```
   🚀 Start Game (Deal Roles & Night 1)
   ```

Now watch the screens:
- **On our GM screen**: Every player's seat now displays their assigned secret role badge (e.g. Alice: *Werewolf*, Bob: *Doctor*, Charlie: *Detective*, Diana: *Werewolf*, etc.).
- **On the players' phones**: Each player receives a private, tactile role card. Alice and Diana see their Werewolf card and see each other's names in the pack list. Charlie sees his Detective card. Villagers see their humble townsperson card. No player can see anyone else's secrets.

---

## Step 5: Direct Night 1

Now let's plunge the village into darkness.

1. Aloud to the room, announce with a dramatic tone:
   > *"Night falls over Millers Hollow. Everyone, close your eyes and look down at your phones."*

2. Look at our GM Cockpit:
   - The phase indicator shows `🌙 Night 1`.
   - In the **Night Stage Director** panel, notice live status indicators for our active night roles:
     - **Werewolf Pack Kill**: Waiting for consensus between Alice and Diana.
     - **Detective Aura Scan**: Waiting for Charlie's target.
     - **Doctor Protection**: Waiting for Bob's target.

3. The players take their secret actions on their phones:
   - Alice and Diana select `Evan` as their Werewolf hunting victim.
   - Bob (the Doctor) selects `Evan` to protect him for the night.
   - Charlie (the Detective) taps `Diana` to inspect her aura.

4. Notice the feedback in our GM Cockpit:
   - Checkmarks illuminate next to each role as their submissions arrive.
   - Charlie's phone instantly informs him privately: *"Diana has a Wolf aura!"*.
   - In our GM Cockpit, the Dawn Review panel calculates the outcome: The Werewolves attacked Evan, but Bob protected him!

---

## Step 6: Review the Dawn Report and Wake the Village

With all night actions submitted, let's bring the village into the morning.

1. In the **Dawn Review** panel of the GM Cockpit, review the computed report:
   ```
   Attacked: Evan
   Protected by Doctor: Evan
   Casualties: None (Protected!)
   ```
2. Click the bright morning button:
   ```
   ☀️ Wake Up Town & Announce Dawn!
   ```
3. Announce aloud to the room:
   > *"The sun rises over Millers Hollow! Everyone wake up and open your eyes. Last night, the beasts prowled the village alleys... but our brave Doctor was vigilant! No one died last night!"*

Look at all player screens:
The dark night screen vanishes, replaced by a daytime sunrise card displaying the morning dawn news. Cheers and suspicion immediately erupt around the room!

---

## Step 7: Moderate the Day Discussion and Village Vote

Now the townsfolk must debate and decide who to put on trial.

1. In the GM Cockpit, start the discussion timer by clicking **Start Discussion Timer** (set to 3 minutes).
2. The players debate aloud. Charlie (the Detective) subtly steers suspicion toward Diana without giving away his true identity too early.
3. When discussion ends, click the **Proceed to Voting** button:
   ```
   🗳️ Start Village Vote
   ```
4. On their phones, each player taps the name of their chosen suspect.
5. Watch our GM Cockpit:
   - Live vote tallies increment in real time on each player's seat node.
   - Diana receives 5 votes; Evan receives 2 votes.
6. Click **Confirm Village Verdict**:
   - The system announces the village verdict: Diana is condemned by the town assembly!
   - Diana's seat in the GM Cockpit updates to a tombstone icon (eliminated), and her phone displays an eliminated spectator screen.

---

## Milestone Checkpoint

Congratulations! You have successfully hosted a full Werewolf round as Game Master:
- You created the room and used the GM Cockpit at `/games/lupus/<CODE>/gm/`.
- You assigned roles to 7 players with zero physical cards.
- You ran Night 1 silently and observed real-time night actions.
- You published the automated Dawn Report without mental arithmetic.
- You led the village through morning discussion and a lynch vote.
