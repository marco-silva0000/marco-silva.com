# Tutorial: Running a Secret Santa Gift Exchange

Welcome! In this tutorial, we will organize and draw a holiday **Secret Santa** gift exchange on `marco-silva.com`.

Traditional gift exchanges usually involve drawing paper names out of a hat—which frequently results in someone drawing their own name, couples drawing each other, or lost paper slips. The digital Secret Santa on `marco-silva.com` solves all of these problems with mathematical zero-spoiler matching, live wishlists, and a tactile **Tap & Hold to Peek** privacy shield that prevents shoulder-surfing.

By the end of this lesson, you will know how to create an exchange room, set a spending budget, gather participant wishlists, draw secret matches with zero spoilers, and privately view your assigned recipient.

---

## What We Will Achieve

We will:
1. Create a Secret Santa exchange room as the organizer.
2. Set a spending budget and exchange deadline.
3. Invite friends (**Liam**, **Olivia**, and **Noah**) using our room QR code.
4. Fill out and save personal wishlist gift hints.
5. Draw the secret matches with one click.
6. Use **Tap & Hold to Peek** to reveal our secret recipient privately.
7. Update our wishlist in real time to see how hints sync immediately to our Secret Santa.

---

## Step 1: Create the Secret Santa Room

Let's start by creating our exchange room on a computer, tablet, or phone.

1. In your browser, open the room creation page:
   ```
   https://marco-silva.com/games/create/?game=secretsanta
   ```
2. Enter the room configuration:
   - **Room Title**: Type `Holiday Gift Exchange 2026`.
   - **Language**: Select **English (en)**.
   - **Password**: Leave blank for easy joining.
   - **Anti-Bot Question**: Answer the simple math question.
3. Click **Create Room**.

Notice that the browser creates our room and opens the join screen.

---

## Step 2: Join as the Organizer and Set Event Details

Now let's step into the room as the organizer.

1. In the **Player Name** box, type your name: `Emma`.
2. Click **Join Room**.

Notice the room lobby opens:
- At the top of the screen, we see our room code (e.g. `3F82E119`) and a large QR code.
- In the **Event Settings** card, we see the exchange rules.

Let's configure the guidelines for our group:
1. Locate the **Budget** field in the settings card and enter: `25€`.
2. Locate the **Event Date** field and enter: `December 24`.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

Notice a confirmation badge confirms the event rules: all participants will now clearly see the `25€` budget and `December 24` deadline on their screens.

---

## Step 3: Invite Friends and Gather Wishlists

Now let's bring our three friends into the room.

1. Show the room QR code on your screen, or share the link:
   ```
   https://marco-silva.com/games/3F82E119/
   ```
2. Have **Liam**, **Olivia**, and **Noah** open the link on their phones, type their names, and tap **Join Room**.

Look at the **Connected Participants** roster on your screen:
Notice that all four participants appear with distinct colors and avatars:
- `Emma (Organizer)`
- `Liam`
- `Olivia`
- `Noah`

---

## Step 4: Write and Save Your Wishlist

Before drawing names, everyone should provide gift ideas so nobody has to guess what to buy.

1. Scroll down to the **My Wishlist & Preferences** section on your screen.
2. In the text area, type a few gift hints:
   ```
   Dark roast coffee beans, cozy wool socks, sci-fi paperbacks, or citrus scented candles.
   ```
3. Click the blue button:
   ```
   💾 Save My Wishlist
   ```

Notice the green confirmation badge appears: `✓ Wishlist saved!`.
Liam, Olivia, and Noah do the same on their phones, each saving their own private preferences.

---

## Step 5: Draw Secret Matches

With all 4 participants connected and wishlists saved, we are ready to draw the secret assignments.

1. As the organizer, locate the primary purple action button:
   ```
   🎲 Draw Secret Matches
   ```
2. Click **Draw Secret Matches**.

Now look at the screen:
- The lobby transitions instantly into the **Matched Gift Exchange** view.
- Notice that **NO assignments are announced publicly**. Even if you have the screen projected on a living room TV, no one can see who was paired with whom.
- The server guarantees that nobody drew themselves, and every participant has exactly one secret recipient.

---

## Step 6: Use "Tap & Hold to Peek" to View Your Target

Now comes the fun part: discovering who you are buying a gift for!

1. Look at the center of your screen. You will see an elegant **wax-sealed gift envelope** labeled:
   ```
   🔒 Confidential — Press & Hold to Peek
   ```
2. **Press and hold down** on the envelope with your thumb (on a phone) or left mouse button (on a computer):
   - Notice that the wax seal breaks and the envelope smoothly slides open!
   - Inside, you see your secret recipient's name:
     `🎁 You are the Secret Santa for: Liam`
   - Below Liam's name, you see his exact wishlist notes:
     `"French press coffee, graphic novels, spicy hot sauces"`
3. **Release your finger or mouse button**:
   - Notice that the envelope instantly snaps shut and re-seals itself!

Even if someone is sitting right next to you on the sofa or walking behind your desk, your secret match is protected the moment you lift your finger.

---

## Step 7: Update Your Wishlist in Real Time

Suppose you remember another gift you'd love to receive. You don't need to ask the host to restart the draw:

1. Scroll down to the **Update My Wishlist** section on your matched screen.
2. Add another idea to your wishlist:
   ```
   Dark roast coffee beans, cozy wool socks, sci-fi paperbacks, or loose-leaf Earl Grey tea.
   ```
3. Click **Update Wishlist**.

Notice the green status confirms: `✓ Wishlist updated!`.
Behind the scenes, whoever drew Emma as their target (for example, Noah) will see Emma's new hint appear on his card the very next time he peeks at his envelope!

---
