# Word Impostor: Social Deduction Word Game

Word Impostor (inspired by *Undercover* and *Spyfall*) is a psychological party game of semantic bluffing, deduction, and wordplay. Loyal Citizens must detect the Impostor hiding among them, while the Impostor attempts to blend in and deduce the Citizen secret word.

---

## 1. Reference

### Game Specifications
- **Player Count**: 3 to 12+ players.
- **Match Duration**: 5–15 minutes per round.
- **Roles**:
  - `citizen`: Knows the secret Citizen Word. Aims to uncover the Impostor.
  - `impostor`: Knows a slightly different word (or blank card). Aims to blend in and steal the Citizen word.
  - `poisoned_citizen` (*Optional*): Loyal citizen given a skewed third word without knowing they are poisoned.

### Poison Modes
- `undercover_blind`: Impostor receives a similar word without knowing they are the Impostor.
- `undercover_informed`: Impostor knows they are the Impostor and knows their word is different.
- `poisoned_citizen`: One Citizen is given a distorted variant word, creating hilarious confusion.
- `classic_blank`: Impostor receives a blank card (Mr. White) and must improvise completely blind.

### Scoring & Victory
- **Citizens Win**: If they correctly vote out the Impostor and survive the Impostor's steal attempt.
- **Impostor Wins**:
  - If the Citizens accidentally eliminate loyal Citizens until only 2 players remain.
  - Or if the Impostor successfully guesses the Citizen word during the **Lethal Word Steal**!

---

## 2. Explanation

### Why Semantic Proximity Matters
The fun of Word Impostor hinges on carefully curated word pairs with precise semantic overlap (e.g. *Guitar* vs *Violin*, *Croissant* vs *Bagel*, *Vampire* vs *Zombie*):
- If clues are too specific ("It has 6 strings"), the Impostor immediately learns the word.
- If clues are too vague ("It is made of wood"), Citizens suspect each other of being Impostors!

---

## 3. How-To Guides

### How to Add Custom Word Pairs
1. In the game lobby, open the **Match Settings** panel.
2. Under "Custom Word Pairs", enter a Citizen Word and Impostor Word (e.g. `Batman, Iron Man`).
3. Click **Add Pair**. Your custom pair is placed directly into the room's active rotation!
